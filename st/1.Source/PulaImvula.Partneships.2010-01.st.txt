

Partneships (Maize) 									SESOTHO

										

Setswalle se simollotse nakong ya 2008/2009



Instructions: 

Folio strap: Lenaneo la ntshetsopele ya molemi

Byline: Jane McPherson, manejara wa Lenaneo la Grain SA la Ntshetsopele ya Molemi



Jwalo ka ha ho boletswe pejana, ntshetsopele ya molemi ha se mosebetsi o bonolo o ka etswang ka ho panya ha leihlo – ke ntho e kenyelletsang batho ba bangata, ba fapaneng, boitshwaro le nako e ngata. Re sitwa ho etsa sena re le bang, mme ke ka baka lena re thabelang tshehetso le ho kena ka setotswana ha batho ba bang ba bangata. Lemong sena re entse setswalle le ba latelang:



Eastern Cape

Lebatoweng la Eastern Cape, setswalle se entswe le batho kapa mekgatlo ena e latelang: 

Dohne Agricultural Research Station – thuso mabapi le diteko tsa mobu; 

PANNAR Seed – ya fana ka dipeo le ditshingwana tsa diteko;

Uvimba Financing Bank – ditjhelete baleming ba tswelang pele Eastern Cape;

Eastern Cape Development Corporation – ditjhelete baleming ba tswelang pele; 

Monsanto Seed and Chemicals – mabapi le dipontsho tsa diteko;

Lefapha la Temo – diteko tsa ho jala, ho tshwarwa ha dithuto tsa kwetliso;

UAP – thuso ka dikhemikhale tsa diteko;

Sidi Parani – thuso ka manyolo mabapi le diteko;

Lefapha la Ntshetsopele ya Setjhaba – diprojeke tsa temo Eastern Cape;

Traction Centre – tshehetso le keletso ho balemi ba ntseng ba sebedisa diphoofolo tse panwang; 

Masepala wa Setereke sa Amathole. 



Mpumalanga

Mpumalanga, setswalle se setle se ile sa tiiswa le batho kapa mekgatlo ena e latelang: 

Lefapha la Temo – diteko tsa ho jala, le ho tshwarwa ha dithuto tsa kwetliso;

MADC – thuso ya ditjhelete ho balemi ba tswelang pele;

Reggie Mchunu, Pannar – peo ya diteko le ho kena ka setotswana Matsatsing a Balemi; 

Afgri – tshebedisano ditsheng tsa diteko;

Omnia – ho kena ka setotswana Matsatsing a Balemi; 

Bayer – ho kena ka setotswana Matsatsing a Balemi;

Shadrack Mabuza, Monsanto – ho kena ka setotswana Matsatsing a Balemi;

Qwemico – ho kena ka setotswana Matsatsing a Balemi le keletso mabapi le dikhemikhale; 

Bongani Nkosi – Ditshebeletso tsa Temo tsa Agro;

Kenneth Mokoena – Sasol Nitro;

Raymond Mnguni, Phillip Khoza, Obet Gininda and Snukie Mdluli – MADC;

Bongiwe Magagula – Banka ya Naha (Land Bank);

Phonnie Du Toit – Institjhute ya Dijothollo ya ARC;

Karel Taljaard – Kalaka le Gypsum ya Afrika Borwa. 



North West

Lebatoweng la North West, setswalle se ile sa tiiswa le batho kapa mekgatlo e latelang:

Lefapha la Temo Gauteng;

Monsanto – peo ya diteko le tshebedisano nakong ya Matsatsi a Balemi;

LED Randfontein – tshehetso nakong ya Matsatsi a Balemi;

Barlow world – ho kena ka setotswana Matsatsing a Balemi; 

Tsunami – ho kena ka setotswana ditekong tsa dipontsho; 

Senwes – ho thusa balemi ka ditjhelete; 

Nerpo – karolelano ya Matsatsi a Balemi; 

Land Bank – ho thusa balemi ka ditjhelete le karolelano ya Matsatsi a Balemi; 

Agricol – dipeo tsa diteko tsa dipontsho; 

Pioneer – peo mabapi le diteko tsa dipontsho; 

Khula – ho kenya letsoho nakong ya matsatsi a Balemi. 



Foreisetata

Foreisetata, setswalle se setle se bile teng le batho kapa mekgatlo e latelang:

Lucas Serage wa Sasol Fertilisers – manyolo mabapi le diteko tse etswang le ho kena ka setotswana nakong ya Matsatsi a Balemi; 

Div Bosman wa Omnia – tshehetso ka diteko tsa mobu le dikgothaletso tsa manyolo, tshehetso ho balemi ba tswetseng pele; 

Natis van Niekerk wa Wenkem – tshehetso ho balemi ba tswetseng pele mabapi le dikhemikhale; 

Deon van der Ross, OVK – ditjhelete ho balemi ba boemo bo hodimo; 

Lefapha la Temo – kwetliso le tshehetso ho balemi;

Novon Protecta – dikhemikhale le tshehetso ho balemi ba boemo bo hodimo; 

Charles Matlou wa Pioneer – tshehetso ho dihlopha tsa boithuto le balemi ba boemo bo hodimo; 

Kobus Lindeque, Gert Heyns, Bright Breytenbach le Dudu Mashile wa Monsanto – tshehetso ho balemi ba boemo bo hodimo, peo ya diteko tsa dipontsho nakong ya Matsatsi a Balemi;

Ferdie Roodt le Jan Davel ba VKB – thuso ka ditjhelete baleming ba boemo bo hodimo; 

Jan du Preez wa Opticrop – tlhaloso ya tlhophollo ya manyolo le mobu; 

Cliffe Deacon from Agricol – peo.
